Real Volume Growth Index — Base Year = 100 iSets a base year to 100 and tracks how sales volume has grown or shrunk each year after stripping out annual price increases.

Solid lines = real (volume only). Dashed lines = nominal (includes price). When the solid line falls below the dashed, the business is growing in dollars but losing volume — customers are buying less, just paying more per unit.

CA and USA are shown in their native currencies so the growth trajectories are directly comparable without FX distortion.

CA Annual Revenue (CAD) iAnnual Canadian revenue in CAD.

Green bars (nominal) = actual dollars billed. Dark bars (real) = what those dollars would be worth in base-year prices, isolating true volume change.

Both charts share the same Y-axis scale (converted to CAD-equivalent) so bar heights are directly comparable between Canada and USA.

USA Annual Revenue (USD) iAnnual US revenue in USD (native currency — no FX conversion).

Blue bars (nominal) = actual USD billed. Dark bars (real) = volume in base-year USD prices.

The Y-axis max is set to the CAD-equivalent of the USA scale (using the FX rate in the controls) so this chart is visually comparable to the CA chart beside it.
