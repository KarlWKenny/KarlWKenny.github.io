Rationale & conditions

Strategic context

Lumber diversification — dedicated sales resource

The proposed $240K investment funds a dedicated sales resource to lead Westmill's lumber drying diversification initiative. The role requires a strong technical sales profile with the ability to build relationships and develop opportunities with key OEMs in the lumber sector, specifically Valutec and Jartek.

Recruitment is planned for Q2 FY27. The hire will spend Q2–Q3 building market knowledge, establishing OEM relationships, and developing the pipeline. Active selling begins in Q4 FY27, with first revenue expected no earlier than FY28.

Approval conditions: Management will table a formal business case for board review prior to recruitment commencing. Approval is contingent on a credible pipeline of lumber drying opportunities and confirmation that the Target scenario is tracking on plan. Until approved, all P&L scenarios exclude this spend and EBIT figures reflect the approved cost base only.
